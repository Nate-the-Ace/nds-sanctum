Semantics
Mother Spell: Its Effect Goes With It. Some 
  spells are cast on one spell target, but give an effect to many other spell 
  targets &#147;nearby&#148;. These spells are called mother spells. Under 
  the hood, they work by automatically casting child spells onto the other 
  targets to carry their effect. (Usually, the child spells are non-visible, so 
  you can't see them, only their effects.)
Every mother spell has the semantics: Its effect goes with it. This 
  means:
  Shared lifetimes. If the mother spell is dispelled or expires, its 
    entire effect immediately ceases. (All of its child spells are immediately 
    dispelled.)
  Disjoint children. Every mother spell keeps track of its own children 
    separately. A single spell target can have any number of copies of the same 
    mother spell, and any number of copies of the same kind of child spell, without 
    problems.
Square Mothers. A square mother usually casts 
  child spells onto the 4 adjacent squares, or onto the 9 squares in a 3x3 diamond.
  If the child spells are non-visible, then they last as long as the mother 
    spell does.
  If the child spells are visible, then they can be dispelled one at a time 
    by any square-dispel effect.
  Shared lifetimes, part II. If the child spells are visible, and all 
    of them are dispelled, then the mother spell immediately dispels itself.
Group Mothers. A group mother is an individual 
  spell that gives some bonus effect to all minions in its group (including its 
  own host).
  Shared lifetimes, part III. When the host dies, the mother spell's 
    entire effect ceases (since all individual spells are automatically 
    dispelled when their host dies).
  If the host leaves the group, the 
    mother spell's effect ceases immediately for that group (since its effect 
    goes with it, and it's no longer in that group).
  However, the mother spell still continues 
    to affect its own host (in its host's 
    new group).
  When the host joins another group, the mother spell's effect immediately 
    affects its new group.
Wherever the host goes, so goes the effect!
Spells
What spells are square mothers?
  Ill Winds: This spell remains visible, and it casts 4 non-visible 
    child spells.
  Drought, Justicars' Reserve, Law of the Land, Vale 
    of Peace: These square mothers are non-visible, and they cast visible 
    child spells.
  Mirage: This spell is a mother of 4 non-visible &#147;attracting&#148; 
    spells, which it casts into the adjacent 4 squares.
  Siren Song: This spell is a grandmother of 4 non-visible helpers, 
    each of which is a mother of 4 non-visible &#147;attracting&#148; spells! 
    It ensures proper &#147;mother&#148; semantics that its effect goes with 
    it, e.g. when its host leaves the board 
    or teleports across the board.

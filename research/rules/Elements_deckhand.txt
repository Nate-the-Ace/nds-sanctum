Deck Limits
  Min 30. The minimum deck size is 30 cards.
  No max. There is no maximum deck size.
  Dup 4. A deck may have a maximum of 4 cards of each type. 
    You can’t include 5 or more of the same card type.
  No decking. There is no penalty for running out of cards. The 
    game ends only when a Sanctum 
    is captured. 
    Card version equivalence. 
      Some cards have multiple versions. All versions of a card are 
      considered to be the same card type for this rule. You may have a 
      total of 4 of all versions of a multiple-version card, in any combination 
      you like.

Hand Limits
  Size 5. Your hand size is 5 cards.
  Cast all. Each turn, you may cast as many cards as you can afford.
  Discard 1. Each turn, you may optionally discard 1 card. You 
    cannot discard 2 or more. Some spells prevent you from discarding. Some 
    spells force you to discard your hand.
  Draw to fill. At the start 
    of your turn, you draw as many cards as needed to fill your hand 
    to 5. For example, if you emptied your hand last turn, you will draw 5 
    cards. Some spells prevent you from drawing cards.

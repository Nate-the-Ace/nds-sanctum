Death Categories and Keywords. 
  Sanctum defines four categories of minion and group deaths, with the following 
  precise meanings.
      Phrase
      Definition and Effect
      Examples
      &#147;kills&#148;
      There is an attacker to blame.
        All &#147;if killed&#148; and &#147;when killed&#148; 
          effects are applied.
        All &#147;when dying&#148; effects are applied.
        Non-magical combat damage.
        Combat-triggered spell effects.
        Weapon-related spells that inflict &#147;extra&#148; points of magical 
          damage during combat.
        Monsters' special effects.
      &#147;dies&#148;
      There is no attacker to blame.
        All &#147;when dying&#148; effects are applied.
        Instant-death spells.
        Damage from spells, spell effects, or terrain.
        Drowning in water.
      &#147;removed from the game&#148;
      No effects are applied.
        Ascension, Cessao Spiral, Dryad's Favors, Shifting Sands, Void.
        Jumping Land's mountain 
          lands on you.
        Boardhop spell is 
          dispelled with a group still inside.
        Moonwalking fails.
      &#147;replaced by&#148;
      This is simply an abbreviation for two phrases:
        The target entity is removed from the game.
        The replacement entity is created.
        Recruit group to 
          monster group: 
          Lycanthropy.
        Monster group to 
          recruit group: Devolution.
        Monster group to 
          monster group: Instability, 
          Volgaris.
        Monster group to 
          colony: Civilize, 
          Settlement.
        Town or colony 
          to recruit group: 
          Zana's Champion.
      Exception: Spells that replace a recruit 
        with another recruit do preserve all group 
        spells (for which the new recruit is a legal target), even 
        if the old recruit was alone in its group!
        Jade Gargoyle, Odar.
  Deaths Squander Spells. 
    Generally, spells in Sanctum don't work on dead entities. If a spell's target 
    is already in the graveyard due to an earlier spell effect, the spell squanders 
    (fails).
    Death squanders rest. When any minion 
      or group suffers any category 
      of death during spell execution, 
      all subsequent spells that were targeted on that minion or group in the 
      same turn will squander.
    Replacement squanders, too! When a minion or group is &#147;replaced 
      by&#148; a new minion or group, all subsequent spells targeted on the 
      original minion or group will squander, 
      even if the spell could legally target the new minion or group. Mnemonic: 
      Spells don't switch targets in the middle of spell execution. This 
      applies to the following spells that &#147;replace&#148; their targets. 
        Spells that replace a minion with another minion: None. 
          (Jade Gargoyle doesn't count, because it can't be targeted 
          by individual spells anyways, per its text, so they would squander even 
          without this rule.)
        Spells that replace a group with another group:  Lycanthropy, 
          and dispelling Lycanthropy; Devolution, Instability, Volgaris.
The Minion Death Pipeline. 
  This pipeline is applied whenever a minion is about to die. For example, it 
  is called:
  Whenever a minion receives an instant-death effect.
  In the &#147;Apply Damage&#148; Pipeline, 
    step G3, for a death from spell damage.
  In the Pipeline for One Attack, step 
    A7, for a death from combat damage.
      #
      Step
      Explanation
      E0
      Dispatching
        For a &#147;kills&#148; or &#147;dies&#148; 
          death, start at E1.
        For a &#147;removed from the game&#148; or 
           &#147;replaced by&#148; death, start at 
          E2.
      E1
      &#147;Is Dying&#148;
        The minion is set to &#147;dying&#148; status.
        All &#147;is dying&#148; effects are 
          checked.
        An &#147;is dying&#148; effect that 
          &#147;returns ... to life&#148; is called a dying-cancel. 
          It resets the minion to &#147;alive&#148; status (which causes all other 
          dying-cancels to ignore this death).
        If the dying was cancelled and the minion now has positive 
          hit points, halt.
      E2
      Death
        The minion is set to &#147;dead&#148; status.
        For a &#147;kills&#148; 
          death, all &#147;when ... is killed&#148; effects happen.
        For a &#147;kills&#148; 
          or &#147;dies&#148; death, all &#147;when ... 
          dies&#148; effects happen.
      E3
      Burial
        All active 
          individual spells on the 
          minion are dispelled (even if non-visible).
        The minion is removed from the group (which causes it to lose all 
          of its group spells as well).
        The minion is added to its owner's &#147;graveyard&#148; list. It 
          remains here until the garbage 
          collection phase at the end of the current turn, when it is finally 
          deleted.

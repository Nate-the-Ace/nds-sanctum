                      Spell Targets
A spell target is any game entity that can be targeted with a spell.
For active spells, the spell target 
  also refers to the entity that was targeted, which is now &#147;hosting&#148; 
  the spell.

Spell Targets. 
  The following game entities are valid spell targets.
  Minion. Every minion 
    tile is a spell target.
  Group. Every group 
    sprite is a spell target.
  Square. Every square 
    on the game 
    board is a spell target.
  Structure. Every town 
    and colony is a spell target.
  Globe. The globe icon in the upper-right corner 
    of the board is a spell target.
  Whirlpool. The whirlpool icon in the lower-right 
    corner of the board is a (special kind of) spell target.
Not spell targets. The following things are 
  not spell targets:
  The Sancta 
    of the red and blue players.
  The four Sanctum 
    entrance squares adjacent to each Sanctum.
  Novice 
    buttons and training icons in a structure sidebar.
  Uncast spells in your hand.
  Other active 
    spells in play.
